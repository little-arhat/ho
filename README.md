# ho

**Site: <https://little-arhat.github.io/ho/>**

My Japanese study book. It follows the *Minna no Nihongo* lessons.

What is on the site now:

- **Lessons** 1–7 — rules in EN / RU / UK / ES, pattern diagrams, examples,
  exercises, teacher's notes and vocabulary.
- **Words** — every word on the site by lesson, plus the book's reference
  lists (food, …); search in any language, kana or romaji (a word matches
  from its start; kana and kanji anywhere). Every word has its own page with
  the sentences that use it: its vocabulary example (the one on its Anki
  card), its kanji cards' examples and the shortest reading-text lines, the
  word marked. Each lesson and
  list is an Anki deck to download, and so are all of them together. They
  are rebuilt on every deploy and import into the Anki deck "Vocab": words
  already there stay as they are, new ones are added.
- **Kanji** — a card per kanji (readings, stroke order, words; search also
  finds a kanji by the words on its card), a drill
  both ways, and handwriting practice from meanings, kana or sentences.
- **Phrase book** — phrases to say and to recognise, by topic, with casual
  forms, likely answers, notes, verb conjugation tables and dictionary links.
  Search works in English, Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese or romaji.
  A phrase's card links to the texts and scenes that use it, and a section
  lists its scenes.
- **Katakana drill** — read a loanword, type it in romaji; it tells you when
  you missed a long vowel (ー) or a double consonant (ッ).
- **Reading** — short texts in the words learned so far, each one a story
  and a dialog about the same scene, tagged with the lessons whose words it
  uses; tap a word for its meaning and lesson. **Scenes** are the same at
  phrase-book level, real-life Japanese for a place (izakaya, konbini,
  station, ryokan, clinic…). A line that says a phrase-book phrase links to
  its card.

New class material goes through the `/ingest` skill: drop photos into `inbox/`.

Stroke order comes from [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net) and readings
are checked against KANJIDIC2 (both CC BY-SA, see `ref/NOTICE`).

The language buttons (EN / RU / UK / ES) and the ふりがな button in the header
apply to the whole site and are remembered in the browser; at first only
English shows. The 🔊 buttons play a
recorded voice (VOICEVOX:No.7), or the device's own Japanese voice.
