# ho

**Site: <https://little-arhat.github.io/ho/>**

My Japanese study book. It follows the *Minna no Nihongo* lessons.

What is on the site now:

- **Lessons** 1–7 — rules in EN / RU / UK, pattern diagrams, examples,
  exercises, teacher's notes and vocabulary.
- **Kanji** — a card per kanji (readings, stroke order, words) and a drill
  both ways: kanji → kana, kana → kanji.
- **Phrase book** — phrases to say and to recognise, by topic, with casual
  forms, likely answers, notes, verb conjugation tables and dictionary links.
  Search works in English, Russian, Ukrainian, Japanese or romaji.
- **Katakana drill** — read a loanword, type it in romaji; it tells you when
  you missed a long vowel (ー) or a double consonant (ッ).
- **Reading** — short texts in the words learned so far; tap a word for its
  meaning and lesson.

New class material goes through the `/ingest` skill: drop photos into `inbox/`.

Stroke order comes from [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net) and readings
are checked against KANJIDIC2 (both CC BY-SA, see `ref/NOTICE`).

The language buttons (EN / RU / UK) and the ふりがな button in the header apply
to the whole site and are remembered in the browser.
